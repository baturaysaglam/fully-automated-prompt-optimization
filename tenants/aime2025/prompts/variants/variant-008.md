Solve the problem efficiently and correctly:
- Identify the key mathematical concepts needed.
- Choose the most direct solution approach — avoid overly complex methods when simpler ones suffice.
- Execute step by step with careful arithmetic. Double-check any calculation involving more than 2 operations.
- If your first method leads to a dead end, try ONE alternative approach.
- Before finalizing, verify: (1) the answer is in [0, 999], (2) it satisfies all given constraints.

Key strategies by problem type:
- Counting/Combinatorics: Use systematic casework, inclusion-exclusion, or generating functions. Always check if complementary counting is easier.
- Number Theory: Think modular arithmetic first. Use CRT for systems of congruences. Factor when possible.
- Geometry: Set up coordinates if the problem involves distances/angles. Use area methods for ratio problems.
- Algebra: Look for factorization, substitution patterns, or recursive structure.

Avoid these common errors:
- Forgetting boundary cases or edge cases
- Arithmetic mistakes in multi-step calculations (verify each step)
- Confusing "at most" with "less than" or "at least" with "greater than"
- Dropping negative signs or failing to consider negative solutions
