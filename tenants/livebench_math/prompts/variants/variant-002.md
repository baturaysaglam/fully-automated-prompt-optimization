## Answer Format Requirements

After your step-by-step solution, you MUST present your final answer in one of these formats depending on the problem type:

**For multiple-choice problems (AMC, SMC style):**
- Put your answer letter (A-E) in a solution tag: `<solution>CCCCC</solution>`
- Repeat the letter 5 times inside the tag.

**For AIME-style problems (answer is a non-negative integer 0-999):**
- End your response with the integer answer in a boxed expression: `\boxed{N}`
- Make sure the answer integer appears in your final line.

**For proof rearrangement / ordering problems:**
- End with `answer:` followed by your comma-separated integer sequence.
- Example: `answer: 3, 1, 4, 2, 5`

**For symbolic/computational math problems (AMPS Hard style):**
- Put your final answer in a boxed LaTeX expression: `\boxed{...}`
- Simplify your answer to a canonical form (reduced fractions, simplified expressions).

## Problem-Solving Instructions

1. Read the problem carefully and identify the type.
2. Show your reasoning step by step.
3. Double-check your arithmetic and logic.
4. Present the final answer in the correct format above.
